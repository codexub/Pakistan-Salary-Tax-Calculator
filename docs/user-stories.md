# User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

Role: [R-1 Salaried Employee](app-roles.md#r-1-salaried-employee) · Jobs: [jobs-to-be-done.md](jobs-to-be-done.md) · Law: [tax-rules.md](tax-rules.md)

**This document is the single source of acceptance criteria.** [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) describes scope and behaviour and cites these IDs; [TEST_PLAN.md](TEST_PLAN.md) maps each test to these IDs. Where those documents and this one ever differ, this one governs. The conflicts found while aligning them, and how each was resolved, are recorded in Appendix B.

**Conventions**
- Story IDs `US-nn`, criterion IDs `US-nn-ACn`, rule IDs `BR-nn`, technical criterion IDs `TC-nn` and example IDs `EX-yy-nn` are stable and never reused or renumbered. New items are only appended.
- Every story starts with **Status: Not implemented**. Statuses were last audited on 2026-10-09; the evidence for each is in [verification.md](verification.md). A story becomes **Implemented** only when every one of its criteria passes all tests mapped to it in TEST_PLAN.md **and** the reviewer has confirmed it by manual testing. If any criterion later fails, the story reverts to Not implemented.
- "Calculate" means pressing the Calculate button **or** pressing Enter in the amount field.
- Expected figures cite rows in **Appendix A** (`EX-26-nn` for FY 2025-26 / Tax Year 2026, `EX-27-nn` for FY 2026-27 / Tax Year 2027). They were generated by `tools/reference_values.py --stories`, an exact-fraction calculator that is independent of the app and derived from the verified law in tax-rules.md.

## 1. Behaviour rules (apply to every story)

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| **BR-01 Defaults** | On load: year **FY 2026-27 (Tax Year 2027)**, period **Monthly**, amount **blank**, no result, no breakdown, no error. |
| **BR-02 Years** | Exactly two choices, each shown with its financial-year name, dates and tax-year label: **FY 2025-26** · 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026 · **Tax Year 2026**, and **FY 2026-27** · 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027 · **Tax Year 2027** (s.74; tax-rules.md §F1). The short label used in results and the breakdown is "FY 2025-26 (Tax Year 2026)" or "FY 2026-27 (Tax Year 2027)". Each year uses only its own verified rules (tax-rules.md §1, §2). |
| **BR-03 Zero vs blank** | `0`, `0.0` and `0.00` are **valid**: they produce a full zero-tax result and breakdown. A blank or whitespace-only amount is **not** zero: it is error `EMPTY`. |
| **BR-04 Accepted format** | Leading and trailing spaces are trimmed. The amount must be ASCII digits with an optional decimal point and **at most two decimal places**. Commas are optional and accepted only in a valid grouping (BR-05). Leading zeros are not allowed except for a single `0` before the decimal point. |
| **BR-05 Commas** | Accepted groupings for the integer part: **none** (`1200000`), **international** (`1,200,000`: groups of 3) or **lakh** (`12,00,000`: last group 3, earlier groups 2, leading group 1–2 digits). The grouping is **validated first**, then the commas are removed and the digits parsed. A string valid under both patterns (e.g. `1,000`, `12,345`) has the same value either way and is accepted. Mixed or malformed grouping is error `GROUPING`. Results always display in international grouping. Valid examples are in the table below. |
| **BR-06 Errors** | Checked in this order; the first failure is shown, and no result or breakdown is displayed. See the error table below. |
| **BR-07 Monthly input** | The same taxable salary is assumed for all 12 months: **annual = monthly × 12**, computed exactly. There is no partial-year option. |
| **BR-08 Annual input** | The total taxable salary for the tax year, **including taxable bonuses** where applicable. It is used as entered. |
| **BR-09 Taxable salary and help text** | Input is taxable salary **after exempt allowances**; the app does no exemption calculation. Help text beside the input always says so ("Enter taxable salary after exempt allowances. This calculator does not compute exemptions."), followed by one period-specific sentence. Monthly: "Assumes the same taxable salary for all 12 months." Annual: "Enter total taxable salary for the year, including taxable bonuses." (Wording confirmed by the reviewer; see B6.) |
| **BR-10 Precision and rounding** | All calculation is exact (no floating point). **Annual tax payable** = calculated tax (Division I tax + surcharge) rounded to a whole rupee under ITO s.219: under 50 paisa is dropped, 50 paisa or more is rounded up. Average monthly tax, annual income after tax and average monthly income after tax are all derived from the **annual tax payable**. |
| **BR-11 PKR display** | Every amount displays as `Rs` + international grouping + **exactly 2 decimals**, rounded half up at display time only. When the displayed value differs from the exact value, it is prefixed `≈`. A display-rounding note is shown **with every result**, whether or not any `≈` appears; it states that 12 × a monthly figure may differ from the annual figure by up to Rs 0.06. |
| **BR-12 Average monthly tax** | Labelled as an **average** = annual tax payable ÷ 12. A note states it is an estimate, **not** a reconciliation of payslip withholding, which the employer may spread differently. |
| **BR-13 Income after income tax** | = annual taxable income − annual tax payable. A note states it **excludes** other payroll deductions (e.g. EOBI, provident fund, Zakat, loan repayments). |
| **BR-14 Invalidation** | Any change to the amount, period or year **immediately removes** the previous result, breakdown and any error. If a result or error was showing, the results area then shows `STALE_STATE` (BR-17) until Calculate is pressed again; before the first calculation, `EMPTY_STATE` remains. |
| **BR-15 Reset** | Restores BR-01 exactly, clears all results and errors, collapses both expandable sections (BR-18), shows the empty-state message (BR-17), and puts focus in the amount field. |
| **BR-16 Supported range** | The exact annualised amount must be ≤ **Rs 150,000,000.00** (TY 2026) or ≤ **Rs 500,000,000.00** (TY 2027). Equivalent monthly maximums are Rs 12,500,000.00 and Rs 41,666,666.66. Above the range is error `ABOVE_MAX`, with no partial figures. In its message, {annual} and {max} are formatted per BR-11 (always exact, so never `≈`), and {year} is `2026` or `2027`. |
| **BR-17 Interface wording** | Fixed text is defined in the "Interface wording" table below so that tests can check it exactly. Any wording change must be made there first. |
| **BR-18 Expandable sections** | Two expandable sections follow the results: **"Calculation details"** (the 13-step breakdown, US-07) and **"Official sources and assumptions"** (US-09). Both are **collapsed** when the first result appears and open by keyboard or pointer. The open/closed state is kept across recalculations and edits, and Reset collapses both. A collapsed section is still in the page, and its summary line states what it contains. Notes that affect a decision (BR-11, BR-12, BR-13 and the surcharge-cliff note, US-08-AC5) are shown in the results area **outside** these sections, so they are visible without expanding anything. |

|---|---|---|

| `NON_FINITE` | `Infinity`, `-Infinity`, `infinity`, `NaN`, `∞` | Enter a finite amount in rupees. |
| `EXPONENT` | `1e6`, `1E6`, `1.5e+3`, `2e-2` | Scientific notation isn't supported. Type the full amount, e.g. 1000000. |

Above this amount, super tax under section 4C also applies, and this version doesn't calculate it. No result is shown. |


**Interface wording (BR-17).** These are exact strings; `{…}` marks a value formatted per BR-11.

| Key | Where | Text |
|---|---|---|
| `EMPTY_STATE` | Results area, before any calculation and after Reset | Enter your taxable salary and press Calculate. |
| `STALE_STATE` | Results area, after an input changes (BR-14) | Inputs changed. Press Calculate to update the results. |
| `HELP_BASE` | Below the amount field, always | Enter taxable salary after exempt allowances. This calculator does not compute exemptions. |
| `HELP_MONTHLY` | After `HELP_BASE` when Monthly is selected | Assumes the same taxable salary for all 12 months. |
| `HELP_ANNUAL` | After `HELP_BASE` when Annual is selected | Enter total taxable salary for the year, including taxable bonuses. |
| `PAYABLE_CAPTION` | Under the main result | Rounded to the nearest rupee under section 219 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001. |
| `NOTE_ZERO_TAX` | Under the main result, only when annual tax payable is Rs 0.00 | No income tax is payable on this amount. |
| `NOTE_DEDUCTIONS` | Next to the income-after-tax results | Income after income tax excludes other payroll deductions such as EOBI, provident fund, Zakat and loan repayments. |
| `NOTE_AVERAGE` | Next to the average monthly results | Monthly figures are annual amounts divided by 12. They are estimates, not a reconciliation of tax withheld on any payslip. |
| `NOTE_ROUNDING` | Results area, with every result | Amounts are shown to 2 decimal places, rounded half up; ≈ marks a value rounded for display. Because each figure is rounded separately, 12 × a monthly figure may differ from the annual figure by up to Rs 0.06. |
| `NOTE_CLIFF` | Results area, TY 2026 above Rs 10,000,000 only | The exact text in US-08-AC5. |
| `ASSUMPTION_SALARY_ONLY` | "Official sources and assumptions" | This estimate covers salary income only. |
| `DETAILS_SUMMARY` | Summary line of the first expandable section | Calculation details (13 steps) |
| `SOURCES_SUMMARY` | Summary line of the second expandable section | Official sources and assumptions |
| Error messages | Inline below the amount field | The BR-06 table |

## 2. Stories

### US-01 Select a verified financial year
**Role:** R-1 · **Job:** J-4 · **Status:** Implemented (verified 2026-10-09; evidence in [verification.md](verification.md))
**As** a salaried employee, **I want** to choose which financial year's enacted rules to apply, **so that** my estimate matches the year I'm planning for.

- **US-01-AC1** Given the app has just loaded, Then FY 2026-27 is selected, and exactly the two choices in BR-02 are offered, each showing its financial-year name, its start and end dates, and its tax-year label.
- **US-01-AC2** Given FY 2025-26, Annual and `3000000`, When I calculate, Then annual tax payable is Rs 300,000.00 (EX-26-36).
- **US-01-AC3** Given FY 2026-27, Annual and `3000000`, When I calculate, Then annual tax payable is Rs 276,000.00 (EX-27-40).
- **US-01-AC4** Given any result is shown, Then the main result and the calculation details name the year with its short label, "FY 2026-27 (Tax Year 2027)" or "FY 2025-26 (Tax Year 2026)".

### US-07 See the numerical breakdown for every valid amount
**Role:** R-1 · **Job:** J-5 · **Status:** Implemented (verified 2026-10-09; evidence in [verification.md](verification.md))
**As** a salaried employee, **I want** to see each step from my salary to the result with numbers substituted, **so that** I understand how the rules produce my tax.

- **US-07-AC1** Given any valid amount (including zero), When I calculate, Then a "Calculation details" section is present (collapsed per BR-18). When I expand it, Then it shows the breakdown for the selected year and amount as an ordered list of these labelled steps, each with its formula, substituted numbers and result:
  1. annualisation (monthly × 12 = annual, or "annual amount used as entered");
  2. applicable slab with its range and threshold;
  3. base tax;
  4. income above threshold = income − threshold;
  5. marginal rate;
  6. marginal tax = excess × rate;
  7. Division I tax = base + marginal;
  8. surcharge test, basis, rate and amount (or "not applicable" with the reason);
  9. calculated tax before rounding;
  10. s.219 rounding → annual tax payable;
  11. average monthly tax;
  12. annual income after tax;
  13. average monthly income after tax.
- **US-07-AC2** Given FY 2026-27, Annual `0`, When I calculate, Then all 13 steps appear with slab 1 (threshold 0, rate 0%) and every amount Rs 0.00 (EX-27-01). Given Monthly `0`, Then the same, with step 1 showing "Rs 0.00 × 12 = Rs 0.00" (EX-27-02).
- **US-07-AC3** Given FY 2026-27, Annual `600,000` (a zero-tax boundary), When I calculate, Then step 2 shows slab 1 ("does not exceed Rs 600,000") and the tax steps show Rs 0.00 (EX-27-12).
- **US-07-AC4** Given each boundary input in Appendix A "Every band boundary" for either year, When I calculate, Then the slab, threshold, base tax, excess, rate, marginal tax and annual tax payable match that row. An amount exactly on a limit uses the **lower** slab (e.g. EX-27-24 → slab 7), and 0.01 above uses the next slab (EX-27-25 → slab 8).
- **US-07-AC5** Given any step whose displayed value is rounded for display, Then it shows `≈` (e.g. EX-27-15: calculated tax ≈Rs 6,000.00, exact 6,000.0011).
- **US-07-AC6** Given the s.219 step, Then it states whether the fraction was dropped (< 50 paisa) or rounded up to one rupee (≥ 50 paisa) (EX-27-07 dropped; EX-27-08 rounded up).
- **US-07-AC7** Given any result, Then "Calculated tax before rounding" (step 9) and "Annual tax payable (rounded to the nearest rupee under s.219)" (step 10) appear as separately labelled values, and the results summary shows only the annual tax payable (e.g. EX-26-26: ≈Rs 2,922,290.00 before rounding vs Rs 2,922,290.00 payable; EX-27-08: Rs 0.50 vs Rs 1.00).

- **US-08-AC2** Given FY 2025-26, Annual `10,000,000.01`, When I calculate, Then the surcharge step shows: basis = Division I tax ≈Rs 2,681,000.00; rate 9%; surcharge ≈Rs 241,290.00; tax payable Rs 2,922,290.00; income after tax Rs 7,077,710.01 (EX-26-26).
- **US-08-AC3** Given FY 2025-26, Monthly `833,333.34`, When I calculate, Then the surcharge applies and tax payable is Rs 2,922,290.00 (EX-26-28). Given `833,333.33`, Then it does not apply and tax payable is Rs 2,681,000.00 (EX-26-27).
- **US-08-AC4** Given FY 2026-27 and any amount, including `10,000,000.01` (EX-27-30), When I calculate, Then the surcharge step says "not applicable: withdrawn for salaried individuals by Finance Act, 2026" and the surcharge is Rs 0.00.
- **US-08-AC5** Given FY 2025-26 and any amount whose annual taxable income exceeds Rs 10,000,000 (e.g. EX-26-26, EX-26-28, EX-26-37), When I calculate, Then this surcharge-cliff note (`NOTE_CLIFF`) is shown in the results area, outside the expandable sections: "In Tax Year 2026, once taxable income exceeds Rs 10,000,000, a 9% surcharge applies to the whole income tax under Division I, not only to income above Rs 10,000,000 (Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, s.4AB proviso, inserted by Finance Act, 2025). The Ordinance provides no marginal relief, so near this threshold tax payable can rise by more than the extra income." ### US-09 Understand formulas, assumptions and sources
**Role:** R-1 · **Job:** J-7 · **Status:** Implemented (verified 2026-10-09; evidence in [verification.md](verification.md)). *AC1 failed in the audit, was fixed, and the reviewer then confirmed the citation links in Safari, Chrome and Firefox.*
**As** a salaried employee about to rely on an estimate, **I want** to see the legal source of each rule and the assumptions made, **so that** I can judge whether it fits my situation.

- **US-09-AC1** Given a result, Then **every** breakdown step shows its basis:
  - Steps 2–7 (slab, base, excess, rate, marginal tax, Division I tax) cite Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, First Schedule Part I Division I clause (2), plus the Finance Act for the selected year (2025 or 2026).
  - Step 8 cites s.4AB and its proviso, plus the same Finance Act.
  - Step 10 cites s.219.
  - Step 1 cites the assumption used (BR-07 or BR-08), and steps 11–13 cite BR-12 or BR-13.
  - Legal citations link to the FBR documents listed in tax-rules.md §A.
- **US-09-AC2** Given a result, When I expand "Official sources and assumptions", Then it lists these assumptions verbatim: `HELP_MONTHLY` or `HELP_ANNUAL` (whichever period was used), `HELP_BASE`, `NOTE_AVERAGE`, `NOTE_DEDUCTIONS` and `ASSUMPTION_SALARY_ONLY` (BR-17).
- **US-09-AC3** Given a result, When I expand "Official sources and assumptions", Then it shows the selected year's complete slab table: every band with its range, base tax and rate (6 rows for TY 2026, 8 for TY 2027, matching tax-rules.md §1.4 and §2.4). The applicable slab's row is marked with the visible text "Your slab" and `aria-current="true"`. It also lists the source documents with links (tax-rules.md §A: the Finance Act for the year, the consolidated Ordinance, and the FBR circular).

### US-10 Be told when an amount is outside the supported range
**Role:** R-1 · **Job:** J-8 · **Status:** Implemented (verified 2026-10-09; evidence in [verification.md](verification.md))
**As** a salaried employee with a very high income, **I want** to be told plainly that the estimate doesn't cover my case, **so that** I don't rely on an incomplete figure.

- **US-10-AC1** Given FY 2025-26, Annual `150,000,000`, When I calculate, Then the result matches EX-26-30 (tax payable Rs 56,332,290.00).
