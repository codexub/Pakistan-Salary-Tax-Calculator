# Tax Rules — Salary-only Income Tax Estimate, Pakistan

**Date checked:** 2026-10-09 (all sources below were downloaded and read on this date)
**Status:** Both years verified against enacted primary law. **No rule is unverified, so no year's calculations are pending.**
**Supersedes:** the earlier `docs/TAX_RULES.md` (removed).

## 0. How to read this document

- **Legal authority** means only the enacted law: the Finance Acts as published in the Gazette, and the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 ("ITO") as consolidated by FBR. FBR circulars and rate cards are official explanations; they are used to corroborate, not as authority. Each FBR consolidated text carries FBR's disclaimer that the statute prevails.
- **Finance Bills were not used.** Budget speeches, Finance Bill memoranda (e.g. PwC's memorandum on Finance Bill 2026) and news reports of "proposed" rates were deliberately excluded. Every rate below is traced to an enacted Act (§A1, §A2) **and** to the consolidated ITO text for that year.
- **Current versus historical text.** Each year's rules are the provisions in force for that tax year. Earlier wording that FBR prints as footnotes in the consolidated ITO is shown here only in footnotes marked **[Historical]**.
- **Secondary comparison.** taxcalculator.pk was used only to see how users experience such a calculator and as a cross-check (§C). It is not authority.
- All amounts are PKR. Worked figures are exact unless prefixed ≈ (which means rounded half up to 2 dp for display). They are generated by `tools/rule_checks.py` (exact rational arithmetic) and `tools/reference_values.py`; neither shares code with the app.

## B. Rules common to both years

| Topic | Rule (identical in B1 and B3) |
|---|---|
| Tax year (s.74(1)) | "a period of twelve months ending on the 30th day of June … denoted by the calendar year in which the said date falls." So FY 2025-26 = **Tax Year 2026** and FY 2026-27 = **Tax Year 2027**. |
| Salary rates apply when (Div I, clause (2)) | "Where the income of an individual chargeable under the head 'salary' exceeds seventy-five per cent of his taxable income". A salary-only estimate means salary = 100% of taxable income, so the condition is met for any income above 0. At income 0 the clause (1) non-salaried table would also give 0 (its first band is 0% up to Rs 600,000), so the choice of table cannot change the result. |
| Slab boundaries | Each band reads "exceeds Rs A but does not exceed Rs B". An amount **equal** to a limit belongs to the **lower** band. |
| Rounding (s.219) | "In the determination of any amount of tax or refund payable under this Ordinance, fractions of a rupee less than fifty paisa shall be disregarded and fractions of a rupee equal to or exceeding fifty paisa shall be treated as one rupee." Applied once, to the total calculated tax (Division I tax + any surcharge). |
| Which Act applies to which year | A1 s.1(2) and A2 s.1(2): each Act "shall, unless otherwise provided, come into force on the first day of July" of its year. Neither Act's salary-table, s.4AB or Division IIB amendment contains retrospective wording. A1 therefore governs TY 2026 (1 Jul 2025 – 30 Jun 2026) and A2 governs TY 2027. FBR's own TY 2026 rate card (C3) applies the 9% surcharge, which corroborates this. |
| Non-ATL status | Tenth Schedule r.10(a): the Tenth Schedule (higher rates for persons not on the Active Taxpayers' List) "shall not apply on tax … deductible" under s.149 (salary). In both B1 and B3, ATL status does not change salary tax. |
| s.4B super tax (Div IIA) | Rates exist only for TY 2018–2022 (non-bank: 3%, 2%, 0%, 0%). There is no rate for TY 2026 or TY 2027, so it does not apply. |
| Teacher/researcher rebate (Second Sch. Pt III cl.(3A)) | "shall cease to have effect after tax year 2025", so it is not available in either year. |

Clause (2) applies because salary is more than 75% of taxable income (100% here). See §B.

### 1.4 Annual taxable-income bands (current text, B1)
Formula for every band: **Division I tax = base tax + marginal rate × (taxable income − threshold)**.

| 1 | — (from 0) | 600,000 | 0 | 0% | 0 |
| 2 | 600,000 | 1,200,000 | 0 | 1% | 1% × (I − 600,000) |
| 3 | 1,200,000 | 2,200,000 | 6,000 | 11% | 6,000 + 11% × (I − 1,200,000) |
| 4 | 2,200,000 | 3,200,000 | 116,000 | 23% | 116,000 + 23% × (I − 2,200,000) |
| 5 | 3,200,000 | 4,100,000 | 346,000 | 30% | 346,000 + 30% × (I − 3,200,000) |

### 1.5 Surcharge (s.4AB, current text B1)
- **Text:** "Provided that in case of an individual deriving income chargeable under the head 'Salary', a surcharge shall be payable at the rate of nine percent of the income tax imposed under Division I of Part I of the First Schedule where the taxable income exceeds rupees ten million in a tax year."
- **Rate:** 9%.
- **Threshold:** taxable income **exceeds** Rs 10,000,000. At exactly 10,000,000.00 there is no surcharge.
- **Basis:** the Division I tax (slab tax), **not** income.
- **Marginal relief:** none. Neither s.4AB nor its proviso contains any, so a jump is legally required (worked in §1.8).
- Footnote [Historical]<sup>2</sup> gives the pre-FA 2025 surcharge rule.

### 1.7 Assumptions and excluded situations
- Input is annual taxable salary (after exempt allowances) for a resident individual whose only income is salary. Monthly input × 12.
- The output is the TY 2026 liability: Division I tax + surcharge, rounded under s.219. It is not payslip withholding (s.149) and not the balance payable after credits.
- Excluded: everything marked "Excluded" in §1.6; non-salary income; special-year taxpayers (s.74(2)); any income above Rs 150,000,000.

### 1.9 Supported income range (TY 2026)
- **Supported:** annual taxable income from **0.00 to 150,000,000.00 inclusive**. Monthly: 0.00 to **12,500,000.00** (× 12 = 150,000,000.00 exactly). Checked on the exact annualised amount.
- **Justification:** up to and including Rs 150m, Division IIB charges 0%, and every other charge in §1.6 is either computed or not applicable. One paisa above, s.4C adds 1% of the **entire** income (≈ Rs 1,500,000), which this version does not compute. Showing Division I tax alone would understate liability by at least Rs 1.5m.
- **Robustness:** Finance Act 2026 replaced the Div IIB table (0% up to 500m for individuals) from 1 July 2026. Even if someone argued that table governs TY 2026, salary up to 150m attracts 0% super tax under **either** table, so the cap is safe whichever reading applies.
- **Above range:** no result and no partial total; the app explains that s.4C super tax applies and isn't calculated.

### 2.9 Supported income range (TY 2027)
- **Supported:** annual taxable income from **0.00 to 500,000,000.00 inclusive**. Monthly: 0.00 to **41,666,666.66** (× 12 = 499,999,999.92). 41,666,666.67 × 12 = 500,000,000.04 exceeds the limit. Checked on the exact annualised amount.
- **Justification:** an individual salaried person is in Div IIB row 4 ("person other than …"), which charges nothing up to and including Rs 500m. One paisa above, s.4C adds 8% of the **entire** income (≈ Rs 40,000,000), which this version does not compute.
- **Above range:** no result and no partial total, as for TY 2026.

## D. Footnotes — historical text (not applied)

1. **[Historical] TY 2025 salary table**, replaced by Finance Act, 2025 (B1 p.511 footnote): 0% to 600,000; 5% of the excess over 600,000 to 1,200,000; 30,000 + 15% to 2,200,000; 180,000 + 25% to 3,200,000; 430,000 + 30% to 4,100,000; 700,000 + 35% above. 2. **[Historical] s.4AB before Finance Act, 2025:** inserted by Finance Act, 2024 as "(4AB)", with a 10% surcharge on every individual and AOP with taxable income above Rs 10m and no salaried proviso (TY 2025). Finance Act, 2025 added the 9% salaried proviso (§1.5).
3. **[Historical] s.4AB before Finance Act, 2026:** the 9% proviso quoted in §1.5. Finance Act, 2026 s.5(2):
   - (a) changed the heading "(4AB)" to "4AB";
   - (b) replaced the proviso's operative words with "no surcharge shall be payable."

   The amending clause quotes the old words as "where the **income** exceeds rupees ten million", but the text in force (A1, B1) reads "where the **taxable income** exceeds". The whole clause was replaced, so this drafting discrepancy has no effect; B3's footnote 7 records the substitution.
4. **[Historical] Div IIB before Finance Act, 2026:** the TY 2026 column (§1.6) and earlier columns for TY 2022–2025. They are preserved in B3's footnotes and apply to TY 2026 only.
5. **[Historical] s.4A surcharge (15%):** applied only to TY 2011 and was omitted in 2014. Not relevant.
